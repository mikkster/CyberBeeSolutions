Accessibility statement

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Our aim

CyberBee Solutions aims to make this website usable by as many people as reasonably possible, including people using keyboards, screen readers, magnification and reduced-motion settings.

Measures included

  • Semantic page structure and labelled navigation.
  • Keyboard-accessible controls and a skip link.
  • Visible focus indicators.
  • Responsive text and layouts.
  • Colour contrast designed for readability.
  • No flashing content.
  • Reduced-motion support through device preferences.

Known limitations

This is a new website and may contain issues we have not yet identified. Third-party email applications opened by the contact form are outside our control.

Feedback

Report an accessibility problem or request information in another format by emailing cyberbeesolutionsltd@gmail.com. Please describe the page, the problem and the format you need.
