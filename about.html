About CyberBee Solutions

Specialist expertise, delivered with clarity and discretion.

CyberBee Solutions is a founder-led cybersecurity consultancy focused on cyber threat intelligence, threat assessment and intelligence capability development.

Our purpose

Help organisations make better-informed security decisions.

Security teams have access to more threat data than ever, but volume alone does not create intelligence. CyberBee Solutions helps organisations define what they need to know, assess the threats that matter and create outputs that support action.

The consultancy is intentionally focused. Engagements are accepted where the requirement matches the available expertise, capacity, authorisation and professional controls. Where a broader team or different specialism is required, this is identified during scoping rather than concealed during delivery.

Operating principles

How the business is designed to work

Confidentiality

Information is handled on a need-to-know basis using agreed channels, access controls and retention periods.

Integrity

Capabilities, limitations, confidence and uncertainty are communicated honestly.

Independence

Recommendations are based on the client requirement and available evidence, not product resale incentives.

Proportionality

Controls and recommendations are matched to risk, maturity and available resources.

Authorisation

Technical activity is performed only within explicitly agreed and documented authority.

Quality

Deliverables follow defined review, evidence and acceptance processes.

Serving the UK

Remote-first delivery with on-site support where agreed.

CyberBee Solutions serves clients across the UK. Delivery arrangements are agreed during scoping and may include remote workshops, virtual briefings, secure document exchange and planned on-site sessions.

What clients can expect

  • A clear point of contact
  • Documented scope and assumptions
  • Regular communication
  • Decision-ready outputs
  • Transparent constraints
